Using the map

See a hike
Hover over a summit or a trail line. The hike that reached it draws itself in orange, from trailhead to summit and back.
See a hike
Tap a summit or a trail line. The hike that reached it draws itself in orange, from trailhead to summit and back.
Keep it on screen
Click a summit to pin its hike while you look around. Click empty map, or Clear selection, to let it go.
Move and zoom
Drag to move the map. Scroll or double-click to zoom, or use the buttons. The frame button under minus brings back the full view.
Move and zoom
Drag to move the map and pinch to zoom, or use the buttons. The frame button under minus brings back the full view.
Peak names
Every summit is labelled. Zoom in to give crowded names more room.
Light and dark
The sun and moon button under the zoom controls switches between the light and dark map.
Keyboard
Tab moves between summits and Enter pins one. Arrow keys move the map and plus or minus zoom. Escape clears the selection.
